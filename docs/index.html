About this public edition

Agent domains describe responsibilities, not identifiable people or agents. Artwork represents Sprites generally.

Conversations are fictional teaching examples. Monitoring contains real, approved combined tool counts with their date window. No individual agent profiles, personal records, private source links or live telemetry are included. This site has no analytics, external fonts, remote embeds or live controls.

Knowledge-check progress and motion preferences are stored only in this browser. Shared-host sites may share browser storage; no account or server-side profile is created.
